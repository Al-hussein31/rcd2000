point load, trapezoidal and triangular loads. Some even carry loads that are distributed not across the
entire span. These and many other considerations necessitate the use of computer programs for the
analysis and design of beams using the high level language approach. Some of the high level languages,
e.g. BASIC, favour graphics, hence, the bending moment and shear force diagrams of the beam can be
