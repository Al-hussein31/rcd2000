HOW TO OPEN RCD2000 (macOS)
=============================

Because RCD2000 is not signed with an Apple Developer certificate,
macOS Gatekeeper will block the first launch. Here's how to open it:

1. Right-click (or Ctrl+click) RCD2000.app
2. Select "Open" from the menu
3. Click "Open" in the dialog that appears

You only need to do this once. After that, the app opens normally.

Alternatively, move RCD2000.app to your Applications folder first,
then run: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/RCD2000.app
